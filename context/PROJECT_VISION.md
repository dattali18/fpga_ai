# Project vision

Research question: Can an experienced software engineer without formal FPGA engineering training, aided by SOTA LLM planning and coding, produce FPGA components comparable to professionally engineered components? The first experiment uses a generic complex-valued DSP filter with synthetic data. The owner supplies requirements and approves architecture with a planning assistant; the coding agent writes and verifies the implementation. Amir is the professional reviewer. Measure functional correctness, synthesis feasibility, latency in cycles, throughput in samples/cycle, generic resource counts and engineering effort. Do not claim professional parity without comparable evidence. Research direction after EXP001 is deliberately open. No internal implementations, operational data, or proprietary specifications are inputs to this repository.

# Research methodology

Maintain separate engineering and research evidence. Record each milestone, model identifier if known, environment, elapsed human/agent effort if measured, changes, failures, human interventions, evidence paths and unresolved issues. Distinguish agent-generated work from planner decisions and human execution. Record observations separately from hypotheses and lessons. Do not invent timing, token counts or test outcomes. Compare designs only under identical contracts and comparable tooling. An expert review is qualitative evidence, not automatically proof of parity. Preserve failed attempts as logs rather than rewriting history.
